Enemies, Chosen & Alien Rulers

This chapter is generated from the same canonical v14 content as the all-in-one guide. Repeated ideas are consolidated; source disagreements and version caveats remain explicit.

13. Chosen and Alien Rulers

Chosen — universal doctrine

  • Early/midgame Chosen are primarily a burst-damage problem; normal hard-control tools do not simply shut them down.
  • Burn can disable selected abilities; Suppression remains useful; Venom can slow.
  • Casey's burst progression includes Both Barrels, Amplify, Skirmisher/Gunner Chain Shot, Cyclic Fire, Assault/Ranger Rapid Fire and Reaper Banish.
  • Mag tier is a major Chosen damage breakpoint, especially because it upgrades the sawed-off burst option.

Warlock

  • Greatest Champion means kill the champion first.
  • Sometimes burning minions instead of killing them prevents immediate resummons.
  • Burn disables CCS; Gunner suppression is useful for approaching.
  • Attack with farthest/least-mobile soldiers first if you are worried he will retreat.
  • 1.2 trick: time the final summon death so Stasis can end just before your turn.

Hunter

  • Grapple creates constant flanks and retreating movement.
  • Farsight overwatch does not give the normal obvious movement warning; bring remote overwatch removal (Combat Protocol, Soul Fire, grenades, rockets).
  • Burn disables the pistol ability and helps protect resistance personnel.
  • Fight mobility with mobility and long-range force projection.

Assassin

  • Scanning Protocol is Casey's strongest anti-hide tool.
  • On Haven Defense it may be better to handle immediate ADVENT first rather than spend the whole turn searching.
  • 1.2 changes made her damage-reduction mechanics easier to manage than older versions.
  • Do not let the hunt for her fragment the squad.

Alien Ruler sequence

  1. Bring Psi Stasis, Grenadier Sting/Frost/Incendiary, Assault Stun Gunner and at least two serious shooters with shred.
  2. Scout until the Ruler pod is isolated.
  3. Position the entire squad before activation.
  4. If unconcealed, an overwatch trap can give free damage before Ruler Reactions.
  5. Open with control grenade; use shred while the Ruler is contained.
  6. Put the Ruler in Stasis before it can act.
  7. Move Assault into guaranteed-stun range for the next turn.
  8. Next turn: stun → unload damage → chain another control if necessary.
  9. Officer actions from a still-hidden Shinobi can be used without triggering Ruler Reactions in Casey's older sequence.

Micro-techniques Casey uses against Chosen

  • Demolition and Bombard can remove cover without damaging the Chosen, avoiding the normal damage-triggered reposition while still exposing them.
  • Venom Rounds and Iron Curtain slow movement when hard immobilization is unavailable.
  • Burning often removes important secondary actions; Suppression lowers Aim and can discourage Hunter/Warlock movement. If Suppression is doing its job, do not automatically flank and trigger a reposition that breaks it.

Warlock manipulation

  • Greatest Champion: remove the champion before expecting clean damage on the Warlock.
  • When he is alone, killing every minion immediately can simply invite another summon; sometimes burning/control is better than deletion.
  • You can encourage Mind Scorch by presenting clustered targets that tolerate the effect—e.g., fire-immune/tanky bait—rather than giving him a premium isolated victim.
  • Burning disables his CCS, making approach much safer.

Hunter manipulation

  • Farsight Overwatch lacks the normal obvious danger indicator. Break it remotely with Combat Protocol, Soul Fire, explosives/rockets or other non-movement answers.
  • Bring mobility and force projection; forcing the squad to chase slowly while he grapples backward is how the fight becomes expensive.

Assassin search criteria

Do not spend the turn searching just because she is hidden. Casey's test is roughly: search only when (A) no more urgent enemies are active, (B) you can deal meaningful damage after revealing her, and (C) you can apply fire/control that prevents her bonus-action snowball. Scanning Protocol is the strongest current detection tool.

Alien Ruler reaction rules Casey explicitly notes

  • A Ruler gets a reaction when a visible soldier performs an action in its sight, including moving into visible range.
  • Reloading does not trigger a Ruler reaction in Casey's note; actions by concealed soldiers also do not trigger it.
  • An Advanced Arc Thrower is particularly valuable because the extra stun action meaningfully suppresses more Ruler reactions; the basic Arc Thrower's one-action stun is much weaker against Ruler action economy.
  • Maim is worth looking for on XCOM-row perks when dealing with the Berserker Queen.

Assassin — do not reveal her just because you can

Casey's v1.1 note is deliberately counter-intuitive: revealing the Assassin can give her a more dangerous two-action turn than allowing her to break stealth naturally. Search only when (A) other urgent enemies are handled, (B) you can deal meaningful damage after revealing, and (C) you can apply fire/other mitigation immediately.

Andy Chosen refinements v6

Assassin — treat her like a predictable mechanical system

Do not cluster for Harbor Wave. Avoid adjacent Assault/Shinobi attacks unless they kill: Prime Reaction will punish the adjacent unit and increase later damage. Andy kites her by attacking from farther soldiers so she moves toward the nearest target who still has an action to retreat. Once her per-turn damage-reduction threshold kicks in, either finish decisively or stop feeding inefficient attacks.

Warlock — summons are the real threat

Spread out against Mind Scorch fire. Suppression or burning can shut down Close Combat Specialist. Assume attacking late in your turn may produce several summoned enemies. If Spectral Lancers are active, killing the final lancer by overwatch during the alien turn can force the Warlock out of Stasis too late to act, handing you a full turn.

Chosen Stronghold — damage race, not scout mission

Five soldiers. Andy does not bring his normal Shinobi officer; raw firepower and multi-shot abilities matter more. Clear patrolling ADVENT before walking far enough to spawn the Chosen. Reset cooldowns before the Ascension Gate if useful. Once the Chosen falls, burn the sarcophagus immediately: guaranteed hit chance makes Banish/multi-shot attacks exceptional. When the Chosen returns, if you can kill them this turn, ignore other enemies—the mission ends on the Chosen’s death.

Alien Ruler — Andy’s stun/burst script

  1. Scout the Ruler after clearing as much of the map as possible.
  2. Position everyone; soldiers outside LOS can prepare without provoking reactions.
  3. Delete one escort from long range if possible.
  4. Use upgraded Arc Thrower / Disabling Shot to bank multiple safe actions.
  5. Only spend actions on the Ruler when they deliver meaningful burst—Rapid Fire, Cyclic Fire, Banish, etc.
  6. Use the next stun before the previous safe-action window disappears; count remaining stun actions explicitly.
  7. Do not give the Ruler a reaction in exchange for trivial chip damage.
Complete Chosen & Alien Ruler micro

General Chosen doctrine

  • Chosen appear primarily in retaliation contexts and are effectively resistant to normal crit-centric deletion plans. Early/mid campaign, assume the fight will last multiple turns and prioritize burst damage + damage limitation.
  • High-value burst: Both Barrels, Chain Shot, Cyclic Fire, Rapid Fire, Banish and other multi-hit actions. Mag tier is a major Chosen-damage breakpoint.
  • A hacked MEC/Drone is one of the best “tanks” because the Chosen/ADVENT may waste actions on it. Templar/SPARK also work; healer Specialist is especially useful because Chosen produce unavoidable chip/wound pressure.
  • Damaging a flanked Chosen can trigger reposition, making normal explosive cover destruction self-defeating. Non-damaging Demolition/Bombard-style cover removal can avoid that interaction.
  • Venom/Iron Curtain can slow them; Burning disables selected secondary abilities and often prevents double-action patterns. Suppression reduces Aim/movement and can turn off CCS-style threats.
  • Chosen weapons are generally not worth delaying main weapon research unless obtained very early. Darkclaw is the most notable exception in Andy’s valuation.

Hunter

  • Usually simplest Chosen but still dangerous. Do not let it create extreme range; it prefers to withdraw/reflank. Suppression helps hold it in place and reduces the chance it relocates around your firing plan.

Assassin

  • Prime-reaction behavior is predictable: adjacent units risk immediate attacks; otherwise she tends to move toward the closest viable soldier after being damaged. Use that predictability to kite her toward a soldier who still has an action to retreat.
  • Do not attack adjacent with Assault/Shinobi unless the strike kills. Every extra Assassin attack also increases future damage pressure.
  • Harbor Wave punishes clustering. Keep soldiers healthy because a later Slice can one-shot wounded targets.
  • Her layered damage reduction activates after enough HP is removed in a turn. Once the threshold is reached, switching to other board problems can be better than firing inefficient reduced-damage attacks—unless the remaining burst can finish her.
  • Venom slows her; fire can constrain secondary options. The goal is to hit the damage threshold every turn while minimizing reaction attacks.

Warlock

  • Mind Scorch can ignite clustered units, so do not pack soldiers/rebels together. SPARKs are no longer a universal fire-immune bait in current LWOTC.
  • He often summons rather than directly attacks; most of his difficulty comes from the extra units he creates.
  • Burn/Suppression disables or discourages CCS movement interactions. Dedication can help Assault cross his CCS threat.
  • Assume every late-turn attack can create another summon. Do not poke the Warlock with your final action if you cannot absorb three new enemies.
  • If he enters Stasis while spectral lancers exist, the ideal line is often to leave the last lancer alive and kill it with Overwatch during the enemy turn. Warlock exits Stasis at the start of your following turn and loses the chance to act immediately.

Chosen Stronghold

  • Five-soldier cap; firepower matters more than scouting. Andy usually leaves Shinobi officer out here because the scout role is weak and every slot must help kill/sarcophagus-burst.
  • Pistol perks can make Psi/Specialist viable sarcophagus damage dealers; otherwise low direct-fire support classes slow the key phase.
  • First area has no timer/RNF. Save limited ordinance where possible and exploit door/chokepoint geometry instead of rushing closed rooms.
  • Cooldown timers do not reset automatically at the Ascension Gate; waiting before activating it trades a small Will cost for fully available abilities.
  • Clear or dominate the patrol in the sarcophagus chamber before stepping far enough to spawn the Chosen. A controlled enemy often absorbs an entire Chosen action.
  • After the first Chosen kill, destroy the sarcophagus immediately. Hit chance is effectively guaranteed, so multi-shot abilities with Aim penalties are excellent. A Banish-style burst can delete most/all of the HP by itself.
  • If the Chosen dies during its own turn (Overwatch/Burn), you gain maximum time before the sarcophagus phase. Nice when it happens; not worth deliberately giving another enemy turn.
  • After the sarcophagus is gone, the mission ends when the Chosen dies again. If you can kill it now, ignore unrelated enemies rather than spending actions “cleaning up.”

Alien Rulers

  • They react to actions performed in line of sight, so action efficiency is everything. Rapid Fire-style multi-shot abilities create one Ruler reaction for multiple attacks and are much better than several weak individual attacks.
  • Disabling Shot and upgraded Arc Thrower can buy multiple actions of stun and can stack. Count remaining disabled actions carefully.
  • Keep some soldiers out of line of sight so they can heal, kill the Ruler’s podmates or set Overwatch without immediately donating a reaction.
  • Scout the map first. The ideal encounter happens after most normal pods are dead and the Ruler/pod is revealed under your terms.
  • Do not spend a Ruler reaction for trivial damage. Only high-impact attacks, stuns or essential repositioning should be done in sight.
  • Typical clean script: scout → kill podmate from outside sight → Arc stun → burst attacks while stun actions tick down → Disabling Shot next turn → more burst → force retreat/kill.
Chosen damage rule: treat Chosen as functionally near-immune to critical-damage strategies. They also reposition when damaged while flanked, so normal “destroy cover then crit them repeatedly” logic is unreliable. Non-damaging cover removal such as Demolition can still be useful because it does not trigger that reposition before the follow-up shot.
General Chosen tanking: hacked MECs/Drones are excellent disposable targets, followed by SPARK/Templar and then rebels. A Specialist is valuable not only for mechanical control but because it can heal soldiers and resistance personnel during the attrition fight.

Andy AI / threat-assessment additions v6

ANDY Threat assessment is not “kill the strongest enemy first.” Rank enemies by what they are likely to do this turn, whether they can be cheaply controlled, and whether your attempted kill is reliable. An Archon may be scarier than two Troopers but still be the correct one-action stun while you guarantee the Trooper kills.

Complete enemy behavior & threat-assessment handbook

Threat assessment rule

  • First objective: leave no active enemies. Second-best: leave no enemy capable of meaningfully hurting the squad. Decide this before spending actions—do not put four shots into an Archon, then discover too late that it cannot be killed and the Stun Lancer/MEC still has a turn.
  • Enemies whose first action is often non-damaging (Codex Psionic Bomb, Spectre Shadowbind, some Sectoid actions) can sometimes be deferred for one turn if direct killers are present, but the follow-up problem must be solved immediately next turn.
  • Melee threats depend on reach. A Berserker far outside its movement envelope can be lower priority than a Gunner/MEC despite much higher HP.

Boss explosions

Sectopod/Gatekeeper death explosions reach beyond the immediately adjacent tile; do not end melee turns hugging them unless the soldier can absorb the blast.
